# Safety, Scope, and Limitations

## Dataset Limitation

The platform is developed using NASA C-MAPSS simulated turbofan-engine degradation data.

The data is represented as vehicle telemetry for predictive-maintenance research and demonstration.

It is not real-world vehicle telemetry and does not represent Mercedes, automotive OEM, fleet, or manufacturer data.

---

## No Physical Diagnosis

The platform predicts patterns from telemetry.

It cannot physically inspect a vehicle.

A model prediction does not establish that a physical component has failed.

---

## Approximate Subsystems

Subsystem health groups are project-defined abstractions over sensor groups.

They should not be interpreted as official vehicle component mappings.

---

## No Guaranteed Failure Prediction

A low failure probability does not guarantee that a vehicle will not fail.

A high failure probability does not guarantee that a failure will occur.

Predictions depend on the data distribution and model behavior.

---

## RUL Is an Estimate

Predicted RUL is an estimate generated by a machine-learning regression model.

It should not be treated as an exact remaining lifetime.

---

## SHAP Is Not Causal Evidence

SHAP explains model behavior.

It does not establish physical causation.

A feature with a high SHAP contribution should be investigated as a model signal, not automatically labeled as the cause of a physical fault.

---

## Replay Mode

CSV replay reconstructs historical telemetry sequentially.

It is designed for testing and demonstration.

It does not provide real-time vehicle telemetry ingestion or IoT connectivity.

---

## Assistant Behavior

The AI diagnostic assistant should:

- remain grounded in retrieved project knowledge,
- distinguish predictions from facts,
- state uncertainty when evidence is insufficient,
- avoid inventing sensor meanings,
- avoid inventing component failures,
- avoid inventing manufacturer procedures,
- recommend verification when appropriate.

---

## Safety Boundary

The system is a predictive-maintenance decision-support prototype.

It should not be used as the sole basis for safety-critical operation or maintenance decisions.
